Advertising (ETF Percentile and Multiplication Game)

These two apps use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. This information is collected and processed by Google, not by me, under Google's Privacy Policy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the device).

Google Play Age Signals (all three apps)

Where required by law, the apps ask Google Play for your age range through the Google Play Age Signals API. Google Play may ask you to share or confirm your age; you can decline.

  • In ETF Percentile and Multiplication Game, the age range is used only on your device to make ads age-appropriate: for users under 18, ads are limited in content rating and marked as under the age of consent, and for users under 13 they are also marked as child-directed.
  • In 암기력 UP, the age range is not used for anything.
  • The age range is never stored, sent to me, shared with anyone, or used for ad targeting, analytics, or profiling.

Children

I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. When Google Play reports that a user is under 13, the apps request only child-directed, general-audience (G-rated) ads, as described above.

Security and retention

All network traffic from the apps uses HTTPS. I do not store personal information, so there is nothing of yours for me to retain or delete. For information collected by Google AdMob or Google Play, see Google's Privacy Policy linked above.
